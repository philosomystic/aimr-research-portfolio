RESEARCH

Human–AI Developmental Research Program (HAIDR)

Founder / Independent Researcher · 2025–present

Developing a candidate integrative research architecture connecting nested human–AI developmental ecologies, distributions of consequential operations, recurrent participation, multilevel outcomes, and capability trajectories.

Current work distinguishes present performance from sustained capability across microgenetic, mesogenetic, and longitudinal / ontogenetic timescales, toward conditional design knowledge.

HAIDR Overview · Research Sites & Studies

AI-Mediated Reflection (AIMR)

A bounded research site investigating reflective operations, recognition, participation protocols, and durable reflective capability. AIMR

Study 0: Intensive Longitudinal Case

Independent Researcher · 2026–present

AIMR’s current empirical anchor: an intensive longitudinal, hypothesis-generating case using retrospective reconstruction (0R), external digital-trace triangulation (0R-X), and prospective developmental probes (0P).

0P Protocol v1.0 is frozen. September 18, 2026 is the formal launch boundary; the September 3 probe remains Pilot 0P-01, excluded from the formal series. Tests include delay, transfer, perturbation, scaffold reduction, and withdrawal. Study 0

Research Sites & Studies
