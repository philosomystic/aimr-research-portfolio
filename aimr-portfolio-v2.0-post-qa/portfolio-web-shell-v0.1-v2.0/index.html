HUMAN–AI DEVELOPMENTAL RESEARCH PROGRAM

Toward a Design Science of Human–AI Developmental Ecologies

HAIDR

Studying development through the ecologies of participation that shape human capacities.

A developing research program examining developmental change through sustained recursive human–AI participation.

The program investigates the developmental environments created through ongoing human–AI interaction: when they strengthen or weaken human capacities, agency, coherence, and independent participation.

5-minute research overview →

Central research question
What configurations of human–AI participation cultivate durable human capacities—and how can we know when development has actually occurred?
Current empirical focus

Supported performance ≠ development

Distinguishing improved AI-supported performance from persistent, transferable changes in human capacities after scaffold reduction or withdrawal.
