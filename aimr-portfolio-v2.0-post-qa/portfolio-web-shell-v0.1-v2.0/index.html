Research Portfolio · Human Development · Human–AI Interaction

Andrew Nixon

AI-Mediated Reflection (AIMR) is a developing design science of developmental ecologies, currently focused on developmental change through sustained recursive human–AI participation.

Rather than treating AI primarily as a discrete tool, AIMR investigates the developmental environments created through ongoing human–AI interaction: when they strengthen or weaken human capacities, agency, coherence, and independent participation.

Governing empirical question
What developmental changes occur through sustained recursive human–AI participation, through what mechanisms, under what ecological conditions, and how should those environments be structured?
Current empirical focus

Supported performance ≠ development

Distinguishing improved AI-supported performance from persistent, transferable changes in human capacities after scaffold reduction or withdrawal.
