Philosophy of Human–AI Futures

Philosophical inquiry into the changing conditions of human and collective possibility

What becomes possible, impossible, accessible, or inaccessible—and for whom, through which arrangements, and with what significance?

My philosophical work examines AI, agency, consciousness, development, governance, and flourishing. HAIDR is a distinct empirical program investigating how participation in human–AI ecologies shapes human capacities over time.

AI and society are the arena

The wider concern is collective adaptive capacity: whether AI strengthens the capacities through which people and institutions understand and respond to changing conditions. This is a societal horizon, not a conclusion established by the current studies.

My work brings philosophical and conceptual inquiry into relation with empirical research, design, and governance.
