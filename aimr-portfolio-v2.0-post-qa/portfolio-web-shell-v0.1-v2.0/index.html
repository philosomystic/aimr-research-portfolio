A research portfolio for human development in the age of AI.

The central research program, AIMR, studies how sustained human–AI participation changes capacities, agency, relationships, and developmental trajectories over time—and how those environments should be intentionally structured.

This shell is designed to hold living research artifacts rather than merely describe them. The first production artifact is the Human–AI Development Research Landscape.
