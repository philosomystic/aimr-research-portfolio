AIMR

AIMR studies developmental change through sustained recursive human–AI participation.

AIMR is not fundamentally about AI as a discrete tool. It is about developmental environments that support—or inhibit—the emergence of coherent, agentic human participation through recursive interaction with artificial systems.

The governing empirical question is: What developmental changes occur through sustained recursive human–AI participation, through what mechanisms, under what ecological conditions, and how should those environments be structured?

“AIMR is the organizing research program I want to develop, test, revise, and potentially transform through doctoral work.”
