Wunderhorn Project

Parallel experimental laboratories · Developmental intervention · Longitudinal experiment

Can the generative constraints of coherent development be discovered experimentally—and can that knowledge inform the deliberate cultivation of human capacities under sustained AI participation?

Candidate prospective research program · Musical laboratory active · Human-development laboratory prospective

Wunderhorn is a paired experimental program investigating development under controlled intervention across two systems. In the musical laboratory, Mahler’s Wer hat dies Liedlein erdacht? is reverse-engineered as a coherent musical organism and subjected to progressively controlled perturbations to determine what must remain invariant for identity-preserving development. The laboratory asks: Can we experimentally discover the minimal generative constraints from which a coherent musical organism can grow?

The human-development laboratory translates—not assumes—the resulting experimental logic into a prospective study of sustained human–AI participation. Candidate developmental mechanisms such as differentiation, recognition, integration, metacognition, and agency would be scaffolded and experimentally manipulated at corresponding levels of resolution, then tested across repeated enactment, delay, transfer, perturbation, and scaffold reduction.

The central human-development question is whether organizations first realized within the coupled human–AI system become increasingly available properties of the human participant: stable, spontaneously initiated, transferable, and resilient when AI support is reduced or removed.

The bridge between the laboratories is methodological:

Musical organism: controlled transformation → identity/coherence → developmental constraints

Human–AI system: controlled scaffolding → persistence/transfer → human developmental capacity

Both laboratories therefore investigate development under controlled intervention, while explicitly testing which principles genuinely transfer between musical and human ontogeny.
