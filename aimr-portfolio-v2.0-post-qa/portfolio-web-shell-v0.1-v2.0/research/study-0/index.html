Study 0

AIMR’s current empirical anchor: an intensive longitudinal, hypothesis-generating case.

1. Purpose

Study 0 is the current empirical anchor within AI-Mediated Reflection (AIMR), a bounded research site in HAIDR. It is hypothesis-generating rather than evidence sufficient to validate HAIDR as a whole.

Study 0 is an intensive longitudinal, hypothesis-generating case study of a sustained human–AI interaction archive and its ongoing continuation.

Its purpose is not to demonstrate that AI-mediated development has already occurred, nor to establish causal effects from a single case. It is to use an unusually dense longitudinal record to identify candidate developmental phenomena, reconstruct their emergence over time, sharpen HAIDR’s constructs, and derive hypotheses capable of stronger empirical testing.

The central problem is whether observed changes across the Andrew–AI longitudinal record support warranted inferences about changes in human capacities, while distinguishing those changes from supported performance and properties of the coupled system.

The reconstructive research question is:

What developmental patterns can be reconstructed, and what hypotheses about AI-mediated development do those patterns generate?

Study 0 therefore functions as a bridge:

Study 0R → Study 0R-X → construct differentiation / operationalization → Study 0P → comparative / controlled studies

2. Why Study 0?

Study 0 begins with an exploratory task: characterizing candidate changes well enough to support more discriminating tests.

HAIDR faces an earlier problem: sustained generative-AI interaction is sufficiently new that some of the developmental phenomena worth measuring may not yet be adequately characterized.

Study 0 uses a dense longitudinal record as a discovery environment.

The archive contains repeated episodes of:

  • conceptual exploration;
  • reflective dialogue;
  • research development;
  • decision-making;
  • critique and revision;
  • practical problem-solving;
  • longitudinal continuity;
  • spontaneous return to prior concepts;
  • and increasingly complex forms of human–AI participation.

This makes it possible to examine not merely isolated outputs, but trajectories:

How did a distinction first appear?
Was it initially AI-scaffolded?
Did the human later reproduce it independently?
Did it reorganize subsequent reasoning?
Did it transfer into another domain?
Did it alter later action or participation?

The object of analysis is therefore not “chat quality.” It is developmental change reconstructed across time.

3. The central empirical distinction

Supported performance ≠ development

Study 0 is organized around HAIDR’s most important methodological constraint:

Improved performance while AI support is present does not demonstrate that the human has developed.

A person may produce more sophisticated reasoning, writing, decisions, or conceptual distinctions while coupled to an AI system without acquiring any corresponding independent capacity.

Study 0 therefore distinguishes:

AI-supported performance

What becomes possible while the scaffold is present.

from

Persistent human capacity

What remains available after support is reduced, delayed, altered, or removed.

Evidence for development therefore requires stronger indicators such as:

persistence · delayed performance · transfer · spontaneous reuse · scaffold withdrawal · perturbation · independent articulation

This makes Study 0 deliberately conservative about developmental claims.

Capacity-distribution topology

For each candidate capacity, Study 0 also asks where the functional capacity resides and how that distribution changes over time:

  • H — Human-internalized: increasingly available independently;
  • C — Coupling-dependent: reliably available in human–AI participation but substantially reduced without the coupling;
  • E — Emergent co-intelligent: best understood as a capability of the recurrent coupled system;
  • Mixed / unclear: partially distributed or dynamically changing across these locations.

Beneficial development is not equated with maximum independence. Stable complementary offloading can be developmentally acceptable when it preserves meaningful evaluative authority, reversibility where needed, and broader developmental freedom.

4. Study architecture

Study 0 contains two core empirical components, with an external-trace triangulation layer extending the retrospective reconstruction.

Study 0R — Retrospective Reconstruction

Study 0R analyzes the existing longitudinal archive to reconstruct candidate developmental trajectories, changing coupling structures, possible internalization signatures, and mechanisms.

The unit of analysis is not merely an individual conversation. Episodes are linked across time to examine processes such as:

initial emergence → recursive elaboration → differentiation → integration → stabilization → spontaneous reuse → transfer

A structured episode matrix supports comparison across candidate trajectories rather than relying on retrospective impression alone.

LDSI — Longitudinal Developmental Sequence Inference

Within 0R, LDSI links observable traces to candidate developmental sequences through microgenetic trajectory analysis, including differentiation, recognition, integration, coherence and corrigibility, articulation, metacognition, and cognitive initiative.

LPTA — Longitudinal Prompt-Trace Analysis

Within LDSI, LPTA traces changing human cognitive participation: conceptual refinement, recognition events, spontaneous retrieval and reuse, generative inference, integration and reorganization, critique, metacognitive regulation, and shifts in cognitive initiative.

conversation archive → observable traces → inferred cognitive operations → microgenetic trajectories → developmental inference

The archive is partially observable, not transparent access to cognition. Candidate developmental sequences include emergence, repeated enactment, stabilization, spontaneous initiation, transfer, and candidate internalization.

Study 0R-X — External Digital Trace Triangulation

0R-X is a methodological extension and triangulation layer of Study 0R, not a separate peer study.

It uses externally observable digital traces—such as revision histories, interaction logs, saved artifacts, and longitudinal usage patterns—to test whether changes reconstructed in 0R are also detectable in behavior outside the conversational setting.

Its governing source-selection criterion is:

Does this new source materially improve our ability to distinguish independent development, coupling-dependent performance, and transfer?

construct differentiation / operationalization

Candidate changes surfaced in 0R and 0R-X are differentiated and operationalized against established constructs and the configuration, participation-history, and multilevel-outcome distinctions described in v0.4.

Constructs developed through reflection on the same record are sensitizing proposals, not a fixed confirmatory codebook.

inductive reconstruction → construct differentiation / operationalization → prospective testing

Study 0P — Prospective Developmental Probes

Study 0P introduces prospective tests that challenge retrospective interpretations. Protocol v1.0 is frozen, with September 18, 2026 established as the formal launch boundary. Work before that boundary—including the September 3 Pilot 0P-01—remains pilot / pre-launch material and is excluded from the formal prospective series. The first formal Research Reasoning Probe, P01, awaits administration.

What happens when the scaffold is deliberately reduced, delayed, altered, or removed?

The design uses six repeated Research Reasoning Probe cycles across approximately twelve weeks. Probe conditions include independent baseline performance, reduced scaffolding, delayed testing, novel transfer tasks, perturbation, and scaffold withdrawal.

baseline → AI-mediated development period → unassisted probe → delay → transfer → perturbation / scaffold withdrawal

This creates a prospective test of stronger developmental claims within the same longitudinal case that generated them.

5. What Study 0 is looking for

Study 0 does not treat all change as development.

It looks for evidence that repeated participation has altered capacities or patterns of participation in ways that become increasingly available beyond the immediate interaction.

Candidate indicators include:

Differentiation

Greater ability to discriminate among concepts, explanations, assumptions, or competing interpretations.

Integration

Improved organization of relations among distinctions rather than merely producing additional ideas.

Articulation

Increasing ability to formulate complex ideas precisely and independently.

Adversarial criticism

Improved capacity to identify weaknesses, counterarguments, ambiguities, and alternative explanations.

Metacognition

Greater awareness of one’s own reasoning processes, limitations, dependencies, and strategies.

Transfer

Spontaneous application of previously developed distinctions or reasoning structures to new domains.

Agency

Increasingly self-authored initiation, judgment, correction, and enactment rather than passive acceptance of AI direction.

These capacities are treated as candidate developmental targets, not assumed outcomes. They are candidate indicators within this case rather than an exhaustive definition of development.

6. Preliminary retrospective signals

The current retrospective analysis has produced several candidate signals warranting prospective testing.

The strongest preliminary evidence has appeared in:

  • metacognition
  • differentiation
  • corrigibility

Moderate-to-strong evidence has appeared in:

  • spontaneous reuse
  • cross-domain transfer
  • organization of written reasoning

More tentative evidence concerns:

  • agency and reduced activation friction

These rankings describe the strength of evidence within the case archive, not population-level effects and not causal estimates.

Their primary function is to determine what deserves stronger testing next.

7. Microgenetic trajectory analysis

A major Study 0 method is microgenetic analysis: densely observed changes within reflective episodes. Linking those episodes also permits reconstruction of mesogenetic sequences across repeated participation.

Rather than comparing only an early and late endpoint, microgenetic analysis asks:

  • Where did a distinction originate?
  • Who introduced it?
  • How was it modified?
  • When did the human begin using it spontaneously?
  • What kinds of AI support remained necessary?
  • When did the pattern stabilize?
  • What happened when the context changed?
  • Did it alter later reasoning or action?

This is especially important in a coupled human–AI system because apparent development may actually reflect a change in the topology of participation rather than a change internal to the human.

HAIDR therefore asks:

What increasingly becomes available as human-internalized capacity, what remains coupling-dependent, what emerges at the co-intelligent system level, and how does that distribution change over time?

8. From case evidence to experimental hypotheses

Study 0 is useful only if its observations become increasingly vulnerable to independent testing.

The intended progression is:

0R observation → 0R-X triangulation → construct differentiation / operationalization → 0P prospective probe → controlled manipulation → replication / longitudinal study

For example:

Observation

A distinction repeatedly scaffolded during AI interaction later appears spontaneously in independent reasoning.

Hypothesis

Repeated recursive differentiation scaffolding can increase independently retained discriminatory capacity.

Stronger test

Randomly assign participants to different interaction protocols while holding task and model constant, then compare unassisted delayed-transfer performance.

In this sense, Study 0 is not the endpoint of HAIDR’s empirical program.

It is a hypothesis generator and construct-development engine.

9. Falsifiability

Study 0 is designed to help HAIDR make stronger claims—but also to expose where those claims fail.

Failure to retain or transfer a candidate capacity under reduced support challenges that specific human-capacity interpretation. It does not, by itself, rule out stable coupled capability, productive specialization, displacement, or higher-order reorganization.

Broader participation-history and developmental-ecology proposals must improve discrimination, prediction, intervention, or transportability beyond established alternatives. Otherwise, their distinctive explanatory role should be revised or discarded.

Scaffold withdrawal and independent transfer remain central tests for claims of persistent human capacity.

10. Limits of the case

Study 0 has important limitations by design.

It is:

  • a single intensive case;
  • embedded in an unusual and highly motivated pattern of AI use;
  • partly retrospective;
  • vulnerable to selection and interpretation effects;
  • incapable on its own of establishing generalizability or causal attribution.

For that reason, Study 0 is explicitly hypothesis-generating rather than confirmatory.

Its scientific value lies in the unusually high temporal resolution of the case and in the ability to turn that record into better constructs, better measurements, and more discriminating experiments.

The standard of success is therefore not:

Did Study 0 confirm the developmental framework?

It is:

Did Study 0 reveal developmental phenomena and mechanisms clearly enough to generate hypotheses that survive stronger tests?

11. Current work

Retrospective reconstruction

Developing a coded episode matrix and tracing selected developmental trajectories across the archive.

External digital-trace triangulation

Extending selected retrospective trajectories through revision histories, saved artifacts, usage patterns, and other external digital traces when they materially improve discrimination among independent development, coupling-dependent performance, and transfer.

Prospective probes

Protocol v1.0 is frozen, the refreshed archive and pilot period have been reconciled, the baseline has been captured, and the September 18, 2026 launch boundary is established. Formal P01 is the next step and awaits administration. The September 3 probe remains Pilot 0P-01 rather than formal Cycle 1; no catch-up probes are part of the protocol.

Construct refinement

Using discrepancies between retrospective interpretation and prospective performance to revise developmental constructs and operational definitions.

Experimental translation

Converting the strongest Study 0 hypotheses into comparative and controlled studies across appropriate Research Sites & Studies.

12. Role within HAIDR

Study 0 occupies the exploratory edge of the larger research program.

It also reconstructs the developmental ecology as a longitudinal feedback system:

participation → change in person and/or environment → changed conditions of subsequent participation → further change ↺

This makes the changing ecology—not merely the accumulated conversation—the longitudinal object of analysis.

It enables HAIDR to move from:

lived phenomenon → disciplined observation → developmental reconstruction → empirically tractable claim

while preserving the distinction between discovering a phenomenon and demonstrating its generality.

Its deeper purpose is methodological:

to determine how developmental change in sustained human–AI participation can be identified precisely enough that it can subsequently be measured, challenged, reproduced, and falsified.
