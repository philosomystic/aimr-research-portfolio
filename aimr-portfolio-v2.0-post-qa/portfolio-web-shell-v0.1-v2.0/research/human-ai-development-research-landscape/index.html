Context & Method

Research Landscape

Established literatures provide substantial parts of the problem. HAIDR is a candidate attempt to connect their partially overlapping questions and findings.

The landscape does not depict an empty territory owned by HAIDR. It distinguishes established research from unresolved inferential interfaces: what warrants moving from evidence about a configuration to claims about later capability at another level or timescale?

The ancestry below follows Scholarly Positioning Paper v0.4. The interactive atlas remains a broader organizing aid, not a systematic review or evidence that every proposed bridge is a confirmed gap.

Intellectual ancestry and inferential interfaces

Distributed cognition

Hollan, Hutchins & Kirsh (2000)

People, artifacts, and environments as a unit of cognitive analysis.

Interface to investigate: How does recurrent participation change the capacities of particular components and the coupled system?

Cognitive offloading

Risko & Gilbert (2016)

External supports alter internal information-processing demands and may be adaptive.

Interface to investigate: Which supports produce retained competence, stable specialization, or dependence under changed conditions?

Automation and function allocation

Parasuraman, Sheridan & Wickens (2000); Bainbridge (1983); Endsley & Kiris (1995)

Automation reorganizes human work, supervision, and practical intervention.

Interface to investigate: Which repeated operations preserve or reorganize expertise, judgment, and recovery capacity?

Coactive Design

Johnson et al. (2014)

Interdependence, observability, predictability, and directability in joint activity.

Interface to investigate: Does a longitudinal record of operation distribution add explanatory or predictive value?

Learning versus performance

Soderstrom & Bjork (2015)

Immediate performance and durable learning are distinct.

Interface to investigate: Which supported gains persist, transfer, or reorganize under delay and scaffold withdrawal?

Organizational AI

Bankins et al. (2024, 2026)

AI outcomes are contingent on individual, group, organizational, and wider conditions.

Interface to investigate: How do workflows and incentives stabilize long-term expertise and capability trajectories?

Reflective agency and self-regulated learning

Kim et al. (2025); Xia et al. (2026)

Self-understanding, goal setting, monitoring, strategy selection, feedback, and reflection.

Interface to investigate: Which distributions of reflective work cultivate durable human reflective agency?

Collective intelligence

Woolley et al. (2010); de Rooij & Biskjaer (2026)

Group capability is distinct from individual performance; local gains can coexist with homogenization.

Interface to investigate: How do shared AI sources affect diversity, correlated error, expertise, and information aggregation over time?

Neuroscience and digital sensing

Kosmyna et al. (2025, preprint); Akre et al. (2024); Leaning et al. (2024)

Candidate neural and naturalistic evidence, with substantial inferential and validity limits.

Interface to investigate: Do neural and passive traces add useful triangulation to behavioral transfer and perturbation evidence?

Resilience and adaptive governance

Walker et al. (2004); Folke et al. (2005)

Adaptation, reorganization, distributed knowledge, and interacting institutional levels.

Interface to investigate: How do human–AI configurations preserve or erode capability when conditions change?

Design science

Denyer, Tranfield & van Aken (2008)

Conditional propositions connect context, intervention, mechanism, and outcome.

Interface to investigate: Which configurations support which trajectories, for whom, at which levels and timescales?

Political-economic and institutional AI effects

Brynjolfsson, Korinek & Agrawal (2025)

Distribution, decision-making power, information flows, wellbeing, and transition dynamics.

Interface to investigate: How do infrastructure ownership, incentives, and dependencies shape local participation and aggregate outcomes?
