Human–AI Development Research Landscape

AIMR does not occupy an empty research territory. It organizes a developmental problem that is appearing independently across multiple established and emerging literatures: how sustained human–AI participation changes human capacities, agency, relationships, and trajectories over time, and how the surrounding ecology should be structured to shape those changes.

What this landscape is showing

A developmental ecology of human–AI interaction

Existing literatures study constituent mechanisms and outcomes, but AIMR reorganizes them around a longitudinal developmental object: how configured human–AI ecologies redistribute cognition and agency through recursive participation, what forms of durable change result, where those changes reside, and whether they remain corrigible through contact.
