Research Sites & Studies

Different portions of the architecture require different research objects, evidence, and timescales.

Research sites define bounded domains of inquiry. A study is a concrete investigation within or across those sites; a measurement program develops the means of inference; a prospective intervention is a possible application requiring its own validation. Listing a research site does not imply that a study there has already been conducted.

From configuration to trajectory

Comparative and controlled studies can hold task and system relatively constant, manipulate developmental ecology, observe supported performance, alter or reduce or withdraw scaffolding, and test persistence, transfer, and capacity change. The outcome is multilevel: changes in human competence must be distinguished from coupled, collective, and institutional capability.

Conditional design knowledge must specify the population, task, surrounding ecology, configuration of participation, outcome vector, timescale, and tradeoffs. Study 0 generates hypotheses for stronger tests; it does not validate the full architecture.
