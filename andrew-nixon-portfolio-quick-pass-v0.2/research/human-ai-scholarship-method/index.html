Human–AI Scholarship Method

A methodological account of how scholarly cognition and agency are distributed across a human–AI system.

“Amount of AI assistance” is a poor descriptor of scholarly agency. Two workflows can both be AI-intensive while distributing problem formation, search, interpretation, differentiation, synthesis, judgment, writing, verification, revision, and integration very differently.

The central methodological question is therefore: How is scholarly cognition and agency distributed across the human–AI system?
